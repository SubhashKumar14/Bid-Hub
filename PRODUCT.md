# Bid·Hub product context

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

College students propose and deliver project work; clients publish briefs, negotiate terms and review deliverables. Payments use Razorpay test mode. Live payments and payouts are disabled.

## Brand and constraints

Retain the independent student work exchange identity, editorial introduction, precise project directory and practical workspaces. Preserve the existing Bid·Hub assets, chalk/ink/brick themes, Newsreader and IBM Plex Sans. Avoid a generic SaaS redesign or invented trust claims.

## Principles

Show exact agreed work and amounts. Make pending decisions visible. Keep work acceptance separate from financial status. Preserve evidence and recover honestly. Use familiar accessible controls with clear errors, keyboard navigation, mobile reflow and retained user input.

## Voice

Write as the developer explaining a useful tool: direct, specific and calm. Say what happened and what the person can do next. Use project, proposal, counteroffer and milestone consistently. Avoid inflated trust claims, marketing filler and internal terms such as persisted evidence, manifests or delivery pipelines. Keep test payment disclosures clear. Never rewrite participant-authored briefs, proposals, messages or reviews.

# Bid·Hub product context

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

College students propose and deliver paid-project work; clients publish briefs, agree proposals and review deliverables. The current supported deployment is an explicit demonstration with no live payments or payouts.

## Brand and constraints

Retain the independent student work exchange identity, editorial introduction, precise project directory and practical workspaces. Preserve the existing Bid·Hub assets, chalk/ink/brick themes, Newsreader and IBM Plex Sans. Avoid a generic SaaS redesign or invented trust claims.

## Principles

Show exact agreed work and amounts. Make pending decisions visible. Keep work acceptance separate from financial status. Preserve evidence and recover honestly. Use familiar accessible controls with clear errors, keyboard navigation, mobile reflow and retained user input.
